## Полный промт (английский)

A short cinematic animated film, 9:16 vertical, about 25 seconds, in a flat vector illustration style with clean shapes and soft night-time lighting. Keep one consistent character and one consistent art style throughout.

**Shot 1 (0–4 s).** Medium shot of a man in his 30s in a dark jacket standing alone at a bus stop on the side of a quiet city road. Late evening, streetlamp glow, empty street. He looks down at the watch on his wrist, then checks the empty road. The bus is not coming.

**Shot 2 (4–7 s).** Close-up of his watch face, then a wider shot. A small yellow shape flies across the frame, passing behind him, too fast to identify. He glances at it, doesn't react, and looks back at the road.

**Shot 3 (7–10 s).** He keeps waiting. The same yellow shape flies past close to his face. He leans back slightly, turns his head, and looks around the empty street, puzzled. He shrugs and keeps waiting.

**Shot 4 (10–14 s).** He takes a phone out of his jacket pocket and unlocks it. Cut to a macro view of the phone's home screen. Suddenly a bright yellow bee drops into the screen from outside the frame, like a flash. After the flash, a new app icon appears on the home screen: a yellow bee on a dark background.

**Shot 5 (14–16 s).** He taps the bee icon. The app opens: a map view with two input fields, "From" and "To". The camera pulls back out of the phone.

**Shot 6 (16–18 s).** Over-the-shoulder shot of him typing in the destination. The phone screen is visible in his hand.

**Shot 7 (18–21 s).** The camera rises steadily into the night sky above the city. Cars with headlights move along the dark streets. Above some of the cars, small map pins appear, each with a driver's photo. One car lights up, and a label appears above it: "Ride accepted."

**Shot 8 (21–23 s).** Aerial view: that car speeds down the road toward the bus stop where the man is waiting.

**Shot 9 (23–25 s).** The car stops by the curb. Camera goes to the rear door. The man opens it and gets in. Rear view as the car pulls away into the dark street. The camera pulls back, rises upward, and the image fades to black.

Mood: quiet, lonely at first, then playful and warm. Warm lights in the car, cool blue in the city. Smooth camera moves, no handheld shake, no text other than the two labels above.
